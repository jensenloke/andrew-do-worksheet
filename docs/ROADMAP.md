# Roadmap

The direction of travel for ANDREW. Concrete items live in [BACKLOG.md](BACKLOG.md).

## v0.1 — the demo (current)

Prove the concept on stage at PLUS: pick any SGX stock, watch parallel AI researchers
read its public filings, get a cited worksheet + premium + CLEAR/REFER verdict, with a
human review step and a visible formula waterfall. Bring-your-own-LLM via `.env`.

## v0.2 — configurable & reliable

- In-app bring-your-own-LLM config (no hand-edited env files), provider presets.
- Hard research timers with graceful degradation (synthesize from partial findings).
- Cached document text; faster, cheaper repeat runs.
- More reliable specialist teams on small/open models.

## v0.3 — a working tool

- Broker-ready quotation export (PDF/DOCX) with all exclusions and sublimits.
- Saved underwrites and run comparison.
- Portfolio screening across a book of SGX names.

## v1.0 — production-grade

- Actuary-supplied factor tables replacing the illustrative ones.
- Wordings-team-approved exclusion percentages.
- Audit trail suitable for a real underwriting file.

## Non-goals

- Replacing the underwriter. ANDREW drafts and explains; a human signs off.
- Trading or investment advice. This is insurance underwriting only.
